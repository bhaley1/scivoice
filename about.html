🌐 English English
SciVoice is a free, open collection of public health microbiology tools built on a simple conviction: language should never decide who gets to use scientific knowledge. Every tool — from manuscript translation to culture media preparation, field sampling templates, and biosafety guidance — works in 22 languages, runs in any browser, and requires no account, no payment, and no internet connection once loaded. It is made for the scientists, clinicians, students, and field workers who carry out public health microbiology everywhere, especially in low-resource settings where good tools are hardest to reach. The goal is not to replace expertise but to remove the barriers around it, so that practical scientific work can be done in the language a person already thinks in.
